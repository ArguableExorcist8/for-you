# 💗 "Thinking of you" iPhone Shortcut

One tap on your Home Screen sends a ping to your partner.

## Before you start
Open https://arguableexorcist8.github.io/for-you/app/ in **Safari** and sign in once.
(The Shortcut opens Safari, so you need to be signed in there. Being signed in only in the Home Screen app is not enough.)

## Make the Shortcut
1. Open the **Shortcuts** app.
2. Tap **+** (top right).
3. Tap **Add Action**, search for **Open URLs**, and tap it.
4. Tap the blue **URL** field and type:
   `https://arguableexorcist8.github.io/for-you/app/?action=ping`
5. Tap the name at the top, choose **Rename**, and call it **Thinking of you 💗**.
6. Tap **Done**.

## Add it to the Home Screen
1. In Shortcuts, press and hold your new Shortcut, then tap **Share**.
2. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Pick an icon or emoji if you like, then tap **Add**.

## Use it
Tap the icon. The app opens, sends the ping, and shows "Ping sent!" 💌
Your partner sees a big heart if the app is open, and it's always in the ping history.

Tip: you can also say "Hey Siri, Thinking of you".
